🚗 How to Connect Your Dashcam

Any dashcam with RTSP output works. One-time setup — after that it runs automatically whenever you drive.

01

Buy an RTSP dashcam

Any dashcam that supports RTSP streaming. Most modern dashcams support this.

02

Get your RTSP link

From your dashcam app, copy the RTSP stream URL (looks like rtsp://192.168.x.x/...).

03

Register with {{ app_name }}

Submit your RTSP link to a TrafficGuard operator. Your camera gets a unique citizen ID.

04

Drive normally

{{ app_name }} monitors your feed 24/7. Violations caught automatically — nothing else needed.
